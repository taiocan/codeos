# Doctrine — v9

Doctrine v9 incorporates Doctrine v8 in full and adds the following guarantee.

## Reality-dependent claims

A user-visible claim is reality-dependent when its usefulness or truth depends on facts outside the
software's controlled state. Acceptance of such a claim requires evidence derived from an
applicable production source and bound to the current governed claim, implementation, inputs, and
source provenance.

Fixtures, simulations, generated examples, and protocol-faithful boundary servers may establish
deterministic software behavior. They MUST NOT establish the external-world validity, currency,
accuracy, or production readiness of a reality-dependent claim.

Reality dependence is classified per governed claim. A solution may contain reality-dependent,
simulated, and non-reality-dependent claims together. Missing or ambiguous classification fails
closed. Unavailable, stale, unsupported, or invalid production data is represented truthfully; it
MUST NOT be silently replaced by manufactured data presented as real.
