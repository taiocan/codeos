# Doctrine — v10

Doctrine v10 incorporates Doctrine v9 in full and amends one consequence of D3 (Platform
Baseline).

## Persistence engine

The persistence tier resolves PostgreSQL by default. The approved Solution Charter MAY instead
select SQLite, recording concisely why SQLite fits the solution. Charter approval is sufficient for
that selection, as it is for a tier that does not apply; no separate exception-approval mechanism
exists. No other persistence engine is recognized.

Whichever engine is resolved, persistence verification runs against that real engine — real
PostgreSQL, or a real file-backed SQLite database built from the project's own migrations. A mock,
stub, or different engine MUST NOT establish persistence verification.

Every other D3 consequence, and every other guarantee of v9, is unchanged.
