# Feature Development Workflow Contract — v9

v9 incorporates v8 and adds one F9 precondition: a current passing `project_qualification`
verification record bound to the Charter, project configuration, qualification executable,
Specification Package, and implementation state.

The project-owned executable reports every Charter System Constraint and exactly four engineering
baseline identities through the Codeos qualification protocol. Acceptance blocks on failed or
unresolved acceptance-phase constraints and on failed baseline results. Deployment-phase results
remain visible but do not redefine F1–F9 or create another checkpoint.

All v8 selective invalidation, Candidate Bundle, revalidation, historical acceptance, and current
validity semantics remain unchanged.
