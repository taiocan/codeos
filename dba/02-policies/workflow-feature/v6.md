# Feature Development Workflow Contract — v6

v6 incorporates v5 in full.

F7 may use one complete, progression-permitting `LINEAGE-REPLACEMENT-CORRECTION-1` only through an
immutable composite history containing the failed `LINEAGE-REPLACEMENT-1`, its separate eligible
`ASSESSMENT_INPUT_CORRECTION` record, explicit human authorization, and the corrected assessment.
The correction contributes no progression authority when any identity, pre-existence proof, scope,
or unchanged-state requirement from Review Policy v14 is absent.

The failed replacement remains consumed and historically `DO NOT ADVANCE`. The correction creates
no ordinary review round, re-entry, replacement, closure, or semantic revision.
