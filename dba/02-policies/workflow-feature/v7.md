# Feature Development Workflow Contract — v7

v7 incorporates v6 except where superseded here.

For a DBA-23 feature, F7 requires a current Candidate Bundle created after F6. F7 passes only from a
`PASSED` assessment of that exact bundle. F8 and F9 bind automatically to it; drift requires a new
bundle and assessment. F9 records the bundle identity in the accepted snapshot.

The DBA-23 candidate-assessment lifecycle supersedes ordinary review-round budgets and every bounded
Stage-8 recovery operation inherited by v6. Historical records remain immutable and readable but
create no new DBA-23 capacity or obligation.
