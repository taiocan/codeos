# Feature Development Workflow Contract — v5

v5 incorporates v4 in full and adds one post-acceptance operation.

## Post-Acceptance Dependency Revalidation

`POST_ACCEPTANCE_REVALIDATION` applies only when a feature has an immutable successful F9 receipt,
its Intent, Contract, Event Schema, and applicable architecture meaning are unchanged, and a
mechanically later change intersects its accepted implementation or dependency surface and makes
implementation-bound acceptance evidence stale.

The operation does not reopen F1–F9. Historical verification, review, and acceptance remain valid
records of what occurred. It creates no R4, second re-entry, additional Terminal Review Closure,
semantic revision, or replacement F9 receipt.

One trigger commit permits one bounded lineage. Current acceptance is fail-closed until that
lineage contains fresh evidence for each mechanic made stale by the trigger and exactly one
independent assessment deciding `REVALIDATED` or `REVALIDATION FAILED` against the unchanged
approved meaning. The packet is limited to the unchanged governed context, original F9 identity,
trigger and affected surface, current implementation, fresh verification identities, applicable
production/runtime evidence, and proof of unchanged meaning. It does not reopen historical review
findings.

The operation is unavailable when the feature never reached F9, governed or architecture meaning
changed, the trigger is not later than F9, the trigger does not intersect the accepted surface, or
current evidence is absent. Exhausted review capacity and an unfavorable earlier assessment are
never eligibility evidence.

## Current acceptance and frontier

Feature state distinguishes immutable `historically_accepted` from derived
`current_acceptance_valid`. Relevant implementation drift makes the latter false with reason
`REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`. A `REVALIDATED` record bound to the exact current state restores it; a
`REVALIDATION FAILED` record leaves it false. An advancing feature contributes no accepted product
journey frontier while its current acceptance is invalid, so dependent feature progression fails
closed.
