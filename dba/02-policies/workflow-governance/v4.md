# Workflow Governance Policy — v4

v4 incorporates v3 and makes Candidate Bundle the sole identity for every new semantic assessment.

Candidate Bundle schema 2 separates feature-scoped governed architecture, product, test,
dependency, runtime, and evidence identities. A changed input invalidates only checkpoints and
consumers bound to it. Legacy bundles and revalidation records remain immutable and readable but
cannot create new legacy lineages.

An accepted feature remains historically accepted while current validity may be `STALE`. The
workflow projects the earliest affected checkpoint and reuses every decision or verification whose
bindings remain current. It never deletes or rewrites a receipt.

An `ordinary` candidate may create a new accepted snapshot after semantic or applicable
architecture change. A `post_acceptance_revalidation` candidate may restore current validity only
when Specification Package meaning and applicable architecture are unchanged. Its `PASSED`
assessment creates no F9 receipt. Both purposes use the four Review Policy outcomes.
