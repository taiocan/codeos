# Workflow Governance Policy — v6

v6 incorporates v5 and adds one receipt-bearing decision to the closed set.

## Semantic Equivalence Receipt

| Receipt | Workflow / checkpoint | Why it cannot be reconstructed |
|---|---|---|
| Semantic Equivalence | feature / semantic_equivalence | A human's judgment that one exact change to one artifact keeps its meaning; no comparison can derive it. `result: equivalent`; requires `rationale`. |

The receipt names one artifact (`intent`, `contract` or `event_schema`) of one feature. It binds
the artifact's previous meaning identity (`from`) and its current one (`to`), as defined by Feature
Workflow v14. `from` must be an identity a receipt of that feature already binds, and `to` must
differ from it. The receipt removes only that specification dependency: it never makes a decision
current that another changed dependency invalidates.
