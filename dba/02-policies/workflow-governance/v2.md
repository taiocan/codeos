# Workflow Governance Policy — v2

## Purpose

v2 retains v1's checkpoint states, fail-closed ordering, command responsibilities, decision
receipts, closed receipt set, and compatibility rules. It permits failed product-journey attempts
to remain visible and derives journey reachability per transition.

## Evidence classes and checkpoint state

Checkpoint state remains derived from canonical state, verification records, and decision receipts.
`PASS`, `BLOCKED`, and `WAITING` retain their v1 meanings. Status is read-only, `check` alone executes
mechanics, and `decide` alone records the existing closed set of decisions.

## Verification records

The append-only `.codeos/06-workflow/verifications.jsonl` remains the sole mechanical evidence
store. Existing records remain valid and readable. Non-journey mechanics continue to persist only
`result: passed`.

A `product_journey` attempt persists `result: passed` or `result: failed`, its ordinary bindings,
the Codeos-derived transition-definition snapshot, validated runtime observations, command, and
timestamp. An attempt that cannot produce valid observations records its validation error. Failed
evidence never establishes checkpoint PASS and is never a decision receipt.

Journey reachability is derived per transition. A newer current attempt supersedes earlier evidence
only for transitions it re-exercised. A failed proposed extension does not invalidate a passing
accepted prefix. A failure within the accepted prefix blocks that frontier and every later
frontier. Binding drift makes the affected evidence stale. An attempt that fails before observing a
transition supersedes no earlier transition result, but its error remains visible.

Acceptance requires current passing evidence for every transition through the feature's applicable
frontier. The human remains the sole acceptance authority.

## Decision receipts and compatibility

The seven v1 receipt-bearing decisions and their formats are unchanged. DBA-9 and earlier projects
retain pass-only verification semantics. Adopting this policy never manufactures historical
attempts, transition results, or decisions.
