# Workflow Governance Policy — v3

v3 incorporates v2 except where superseded here.

## Accepted state

F9 creates an immutable accepted snapshot. Historical checkpoint completion is thereafter read from
that snapshot, not recomputed against later project state. Current validity is a separate derived
fact: `VALID` when the accepted governed meaning and accepted product/dependency inputs remain
current, otherwise `STALE`. A stale feature preserves its historical acceptance and fails closed for
dependent progression until revalidated or recovered through governed work.

Status must never label a feature complete while presenting its historical checkpoints as blocked.
Legacy acceptance receipts remain immutable and are projected without manufacturing new records.

## Candidate Bundle

After F6, `prepare-candidate` may materialize one immutable, content-addressed Candidate Bundle from
canonical governed inputs, current verification records, selected production/runtime evidence, and
the mechanically derived implementation/dependency surface. Explicit additions may widen that
surface but cannot exclude derived inputs. The command performs no verification and records no human
decision. F7, F8, and F9 bind to the same current bundle.

Product, test, dependency, and runtime-topology identities are separate. A later change invalidates
only consumers whose recorded input sets intersect it. Test-only and governance-only changes do not
by themselves stale an accepted product snapshot.

## Compatibility

DBA configurations selecting earlier policy versions retain their existing live-checkpoint and
binding behavior. Adoption creates no synthetic candidate, acceptance, or assessment record.
