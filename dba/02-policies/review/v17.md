# Review Policy — v17

v17 incorporates v16. Every new semantic assessment is bound to one immutable Candidate Bundle and
uses only `NOT_READY`, `EXECUTION_INVALID`, `BLOCKED`, or `PASSED`.

Architecture proposal review is advisory impact analysis: it challenges feature applicability,
dependency reach, omitted consumers, risks, and uncertainty, but grants no approval and consumes no
candidate assessment. Candidate assessment occurs only after the affected implementation and
evidence are ready.

Legacy review and revalidation records remain historical inputs. No new review round, recovery
lineage, or legacy post-acceptance assessment may be created.
