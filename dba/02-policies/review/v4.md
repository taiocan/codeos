# Review Policy — v4

Review Policy v4 incorporates Review Policy v3 in full and adds the following governed exception
to its Review Round Limit.

## Bounded Review Re-entry Authorization

After R3, the human may authorize one bounded Review re-entry. The re-entry is distinct from an
ordinary fourth round: it preserves the exhausted R1-R3 history, cites the human authorization and
its scope, and does not permit a second re-entry.

`AUTHORIZE_REENTRY` is a review-control decision only. It names the prior R3 and records a non-empty
reason and bounded scope. It is not stage approval, workflow progression, product acceptance, or a
workflow decision receipt. A later `STOP` for the same feature and stage denies execution of an
unconsumed authorization. Other stage dispositions neither create nor consume authorization.

A consumed authorization permits exactly one additional reviewer execution against the identified
prior review boundary; it never changes stage state by itself. Canonical review-log order is the
physical append order of complete records. Timestamps are evidence and citation data, never ordering
authority. Decisions for another feature or stage and external assessments do not participate.

No decision is rewritten, deleted, or reclassified. This rule derives current progression authority
from the immutable history and applies only to Review re-entry.
