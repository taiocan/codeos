# Review Policy — v13

v13 incorporates v12 in full.

One eligible `POST_ACCEPTANCE_REVALIDATION` trigger permits exactly one independent bounded
assessment. It decides only whether the previously accepted feature still satisfies its unchanged
approved meaning after the identified later dependency or integration change. Its closed result set
is `REVALIDATED` and `REVALIDATION FAILED`.

This assessment is not a Stage-8 round, re-entry, closure, replacement assessment, or acceptance.
It cannot reinterpret historical findings, add review capacity, or authorize changed governed
meaning. The workflow tool mechanically establishes eligibility, constructs the frozen packet,
and records the independently produced decision against that packet's exact current identities.
