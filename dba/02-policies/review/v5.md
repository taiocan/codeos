# Review Policy — v5

Review Policy v5 incorporates Review Policy v4 in full and adds this governed exception.

## Evidence Closure Review

After the single `REENTRY-1` has been consumed with `DO NOT ADVANCE`, the human may authorize one
`EVIDENCE-CLOSURE-1` only when every remaining blocker is explicitly an evidence-accessibility,
evidence-coverage, or evidence-format finding. No unresolved finding may allege an implementation,
approved-behavior, architecture, privacy, safety, or data-integrity defect.

`AUTHORIZE_EVIDENCE_CLOSURE` identifies the consumed `REENTRY-1`, enumerates the exact findings,
and records a non-empty bounded reason. The closure may examine only newly supplied or newly
accessible evidence, establish whether those findings close against the same implementation and
governed inputs, and report a contradiction or defect directly revealed by that evidence. It may
not conduct another general review, reconsider closed findings, expand scope, review unrelated
changes, change governed meaning, or itself approve or accept anything. Material implementation or
governed-artifact drift makes the closure ineligible.

One authorization permits exactly one execution, whether it succeeds or fails. A later `STOP`
before consumption cancels it. No second closure or implicit retry exists. Physical append order
is authoritative; timestamps are citation data only. Existing records remain immutable.

The closure result is exactly `EVIDENCE CLOSED — PROGRESSION PERMITTED` or `DO NOT ADVANCE`.
Progression is permitted only from the composite history: exhausted R1–R3, consumed authorized
`REENTRY-1`, exclusively eligible residual evidence findings, a closure examining exactly those
findings against current bindings, every finding closed, and no contradiction or new blocker.
