# Review Policy — v10

Review Policy v10 incorporates Review Policy v9 in full and adds Terminal Review Closure.

Terminal Review Closure does not create another review round. It may only establish that the finite
unresolved conditions of a consumed Stage 8 `REENTRY-1` were repaired under unchanged governed
meaning. One closure is eligible when the package-bound R1–R3 and `REENTRY-1` are consumed, the
terminal result contains substantive blockers or is incomplete, the approved Specification Package
and project architecture remain unchanged, and no closure execution already exists for that
terminal review.

A human authorization binds a finite condition set to the immutable terminal assessment. Each
condition's authority is exactly one parsed finding, one exact excerpt verified against the stored
assessment, or the exact tool-recorded incomplete reason. A normalized description is explanatory
only. Every parsed blocking finding must be included. Ambiguity that cannot be bounded from those
sources makes closure ineligible.

Repair occurs under existing Targeted Refinement authority. A repair manifest accounts for every
authorized condition, changed path, implementation identity, applicable evidence identity, and the
rationale linking them. The tool verifies identity, completeness, bindings, and freshness. The
closure reviewer—not the tool—judges whether the repair remains within the authorized scope. When
implementation changes, evidence proving the repair must be newly produced and bound to the
repaired implementation. Governed-meaning, semantic-revision, or project-architecture drift makes
closure ineligible.

The closure assessment answers only whether the authorized conditions were repaired, whether the
applicable evidence is valid, and whether the repair introduced an obvious blocker within the
reviewed repair surface. It does not reopen the feature for general review. One authorization
permits exactly one execution; authorization alone does not consume it and a later `STOP` denies
execution. A negative, incomplete, or malformed execution consumes the mechanism. No closure may
target another closure.

Progression requires the immutable composite history and a complete closure that closes every
authorized condition without a new blocker. The closure never rewrites the terminal result or
advances F7 by itself.
