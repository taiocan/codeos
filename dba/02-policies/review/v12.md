# Review Policy — v12

Review Policy v12 incorporates Review Policy v11 in full and adds Invalid Review Input Lineage
Recovery.

A structurally complete, progression-permitting Stage 8 assessment has
`INVALID_REVIEW_INPUT_LINEAGE` applicability when deterministic immutable evidence proves that,
before reviewer execution, a mandatory governed input contradicted the authoritative input selected
for that review. Execution completeness and the reviewer's semantic conclusion remain unchanged;
the separate invalid applicability means the assessment contributes no progression authority.

Qualification is mechanical and limited to an authoritative F6 production-evidence mismatch, an
unsupported mandatory implementation identity, an implementation/evidence mismatch, a mandatory
governed-input mismatch, or an absent mandatory binding that cannot be derived unambiguously.
Reviewer disagreement, findings, `DO NOT ADVANCE`, exhausted review capacity, insufficient
evidence, and post-review drift never qualify. The invalid record and its assessment remain
immutable and consumed.

After Codeos records the invalid applicability, one human authorization may permit one bounded
replacement assessment. Authorization binds the invalid review and applicability-record identity.
Before execution, the current implementation must have one coherent committed baseline; governed
meaning, semantic revision, and architecture must match the invalid review; current required
verification must pass; and F6 must explicitly select the exact production evidence supplied to
the replacement. Any ambiguity fails closed.

The replacement reassesses the invalid review's exact authorized semantic scope from complete
current context. It inherits no conclusion, finding disposition, or progression state from the
invalid review. Its deterministic identity is
`REV__<feature>__8__SR<revision>__LINEAGE-REPLACEMENT-1`. One execution consumes the mechanism
whether complete, incomplete, positive, or negative. No retry exists.

This mechanism is not R4, REENTRY-2, another Terminal Review Closure, a semantic revision, or a
fresh ordinary review cycle. Its capacity belongs only to the feature, stage, semantic revision,
invalid review identity, and applicability-record identity. An unfavorable assessment cannot open
it. F7 may use the replacement only through the immutable composite history proving every
qualification, authorization, binding, and final assessment.
