# Review Policy — v9

Review Policy v9 incorporates Review Policy v8 in full.

For Stage 8, the existing single bounded review re-entry is scoped to the same review-cycle
identity as ordinary review: feature, stage, and the Specification Package's approved
`semantic_revision`. Each legitimate cycle permits at most one `REENTRY-1` execution. That
execution requires valid human authorization bound to that cycle's exact R3.

Authorization does not itself consume the re-entry. The existing authorization and STOP lifecycle
remains unchanged: only execution consumes the cycle's capacity, and authorization never implies a
successful review or workflow progression. All prior review, authorization, STOP, and re-entry
records remain immutable.

A Stage 8 cycle with no semantic-revision token is the single legacy revision-0 cycle solely for
identity resolution. No historical record is rewritten. Re-entry in one legitimate cycle neither
consumes nor restores capacity in another cycle.

The Review Policy v8 conditions for opening a successor semantic revision remain mandatory. An
exhausted ordinary or re-entry budget, by itself, is not a governed semantic change and cannot
create another review cycle. Non-Stage-8 re-entry behavior is unchanged.
