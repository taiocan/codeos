# Review Policy — v11

Review Policy v11 incorporates Review Policy v10 in full and prospectively distinguishes a
Terminal Review Closure execution failure from its single semantic closure assessment.

A closure response is a semantic assessment only when its parse status is `OK` and its assessment
status is `COMPLETE`. A complete result that leaves any authorized condition open is the final
`OPEN` judgment. A complete result closing every condition without a new in-scope blocker is the
final `CLOSED` judgment. Either judgment consumes the one semantic closure opportunity.

A malformed or structurally incomplete response is an execution failure. It is preserved
immutably, has no `OPEN` or `CLOSED` disposition, contributes no substantive meaning to F7, and
does not consume the original human authorization or the semantic closure. At most one bridge
retry may follow. A malformed retry is preserved and exhausts retry capacity; no third execution
is permitted.

The retry uses the identical decision scope, repair scope, governed inputs, implementation, and
architecture. Its evidence is also identical unless the incomplete attempt itself identifies an
evidence representation deficiency that prevents assessment of an authorized condition. In that
case, one `ASSESSABILITY_CORRECTION` may replace or supplement only the identified evidence with
inspectable evidence bound to the same implementation and evidence lineage. It must cite the exact
deficiency in the immutable failed assessment, preserve the superseded evidence and failed attempt,
and must not add a closure condition, product change, or unrelated evidence. The correction changes
what can be inspected, not what may be decided.

The control-plane configuration may change only from the historical governing DBA to DBA-18 to
establish retry eligibility. Any implementation, governed-meaning, architecture, closure-scope, or
unclassified evidence change makes retry ineligible. The retry reassesses the complete original
closure scope because an incomplete attempt establishes no partial semantic disposition.

A preserved DBA-17 closure record may use the legacy bridge only when immutable history proves a
valid authorization for the same package cycle, a structurally malformed or incomplete result,
absence of a complete semantic disposition, unchanged bindings, and no later semantic closure or
bridge retry. Complete DBA-17 `OPEN` or `CLOSED` judgments never qualify. All prior history remains
unchanged.
