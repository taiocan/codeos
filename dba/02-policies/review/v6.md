# Review Policy — v6

Review Policy v6 incorporates Review Policy v5 in full and adds one terminal Infrastructure Repair
Revalidation.

It is eligible only when R1–R3, `REENTRY-1`, and `EVIDENCE-CLOSURE-1` are exhausted; the closure
remains blocked solely by named evidence-system findings; CodeOS subsequently records repaired
canonical evidence; the reviewed implementation and governed product artifacts are unchanged; and
a human explicitly authorizes revalidation of those findings against that evidence.

The revalidation examines only whether the named evidence findings close. It may report a product
defect directly revealed by the evidence. It may not perform a general review, reconsider unrelated
findings, repair implementation, alter approved meaning or architecture, create another re-entry or
closure, replace F8/F9, approve a stage, accept a feature, or override F7.

One authorization permits exactly one execution for the identified exhausted history and
infrastructure-repair evidence, whether positive or negative. A negative result remains negative.
Existing decisions and reviews retain their outcome, validity, and meaning.

F7 may progress only from the immutable composite history proving the exhausted review mechanisms,
the named evidence-system blocker, canonical repaired evidence bound to the same implementation and
governed inputs, exact human authorization, a complete bounded revalidation, closure of every named
finding, and no new blocker. The revalidation record never changes F7 by itself.
