# Review Policy — v2

## Default Review Timing

Advisory review runs by default immediately before each decision boundary required by the selected
doctrine and before a conditional architecture decision required by the selected architecture
policy. Review does not create a decision boundary of its own.

## Reviewer Independence

The reviewer is independent, read-only,
and non-gatekeeping — its verdict (NO OBJECTION / CHANGES ADVISED / DO NOT ADVANCE) informs the
owner of the applicable boundary but never auto-blocks or completes that boundary.

## Review Round Limit

**Round budget.** Round 1 runs before the applicable boundary. Rounds 2-3 are allowed for fixes or material
deltas raised by the previous round. After 3 rounds, stop and require a human decision rather
than continuing to iterate automatically.

## Solution Discovery Review

**Solution Discovery is reviewed conditionally, not unconditionally.** The Discovery session
itself stays optional and non-authoritative — running it is never required, and its output is
never an approved architecture. If its output is carried into governed artifacts, the claim is
reviewed at the next applicable review point. A Discovery session whose output nobody acts on is
simply never reviewed.

## Review Waiver

**Review Waiver.** If reviewer tooling is unavailable or not configured for this project, the
human records an explicit waiver with a reason and may continue — skipping the review silently is
not allowed, and neither is blocking the whole project over missing reviewer setup.

Record it as a plain entry in that feature's review log: "Review waiver: reviewer not
configured for this project; proceeding without advisory review at `<stage>`. Reason:
`<text>`."

## Verification-Only Evidence

**Verification round-trip.** When a reviewer's assessment names a `HIGHEST-IMPACT
UNCERTAINTY` that is mechanically checkable — a specific file, command, or repository state
that can directly confirm or refute it — the acting agent may run
`.codeos/toolkit/dba/03-prompts/review/verify-only.md`'s read-only verification pass targeting exactly
that uncertainty, then attach its Verification-Only Report as evidence for the next review
round. This is optional and
judged by the acting agent, not automatic or mandatory — not every uncertainty is
mechanically checkable, and declining to run it is always a valid choice. Verification
remains strictly read-only (see `verify-only.md`'s No-Edit Rule); it produces evidence for
the human's decision, it does not replace it. A verification pass is not itself a review
round and does not consume the round budget above — only the review round it feeds into does.

## Supplementary Independent Review

**Relationship to the Reviewer Activation Package**
(`.codeos/toolkit/dba/03-prompts/review/pipeline-reviewer.md`).
That prompt remains available as an **optional, supplementary** independent critical-assessor
pass — a human can paste it into a separate reviewer session for a second opinion that is
free to challenge the artifact, the feature, or DBA itself, not just check it against
acceptance criteria. It does not replace the default review above.

## Automated Review Record Ownership

The reviewer tool is the single authoritative owner of automated review records. Its supported
review and decision operations record the automated assessment and associated human decision
without requiring Claude or the project to maintain a synchronized per-feature review log.

This policy defines record ownership, not storage topology. Feature-specific automated review
history may be obtained by filtering tool-owned records using feature, change, or review identifiers
already present in those records. Do not introduce a new index, identifier, or copied feature log
merely to make filtering easier. If reliable filtering is not currently possible, leave it that way
until a demonstrated need justifies a change.

The reviewer tool's decision operation records a human decision associated with an automated
review; it does not acquire approval authority. The applicable doctrine boundary remains the owner
of approval.

## Other Human Review Decisions

A human decision not produced through the automated reviewer is recorded at the governing artifact
or approval boundary that naturally owns it. Do not create an automated-review-shaped record or a
parallel synchronization surface for it.

## Review-Process Measurement

`dba/05-guidance/templates/review-file.md` is an optional, non-authoritative template for evaluating
review effectiveness and cost. It may reference tool-owned records by their existing identifiers,
but it must not copy or independently maintain their findings, verdicts, approval state, lifecycle
state, or history. Measurements that can be calculated from tool-owned records are derived from
those records rather than maintained as a second source of facts.
