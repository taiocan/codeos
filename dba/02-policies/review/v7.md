# Review Policy — v7

Review Policy v7 incorporates Review Policy v4 in full and adds the normal F7 Evidence Repair
Loop. Review Policies v5 and v6 remain authoritative for configurations that select them; v7 does
not permit new Evidence Closure or Infrastructure Repair Revalidation authorizations.

## Evidence Repair Loop

An F7 blocker is `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` only when it states that available evidence does not prove
an approved claim and does not allege a defect in implementation, approved meaning, architecture,
safety, integrity, or another governed input. Every other blocker is `SUBSTANTIVE`.

The loop is eligible only while every currently blocking finding is `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` and the
reviewed implementation and governed inputs remain unchanged. Any substantive blocker makes F7
ineligible for evidence-only progression and routes through the existing lifecycle appropriate to
that finding. The review that records eligible findings opens the loop; no separate human
authorization is required.

Each reassessment is limited to named open evidence findings and new canonical evidence. It does
not consume or reset R1-R3, approve a stage, change product state, or reinterpret an earlier
decision. The engine establishes identity, current bindings, exact scope, and non-equivalence. The
reviewer alone judges whether the evidence materially addresses the finding and whether it closes.

Equivalent evidence cannot trigger another complete reassessment. A complete reassessment may
leave a finding open only when it records how materially new evidence addressed the finding and
what proof remains absent. A malformed or incomplete assessment closes nothing and establishes no
semantic assessment of the supplied evidence.

Implementation or governed-input drift, or a substantive blocker discovered during reassessment,
closes the evidence loop for that review history. The substantive finding is recorded and routes
through its existing applicable lifecycle; the triggering evidence cannot open another evidence
reassessment.

F7 progresses only from the immutable composite history proving that every open evidence finding
closed and that no substantive blocker exists.

## DBA-13 Forward Bridge

A successor configuration may derive initial open evidence findings from a negative, fully valid
DBA-13 `INFRA-REVALIDATION-1`. The bridge carries only its named unresolved evidence findings. It
does not alter the record's `DO NOT ADVANCE` result, validity, or meaning. The complete prior chain,
feature and stage, canonical append order, and unchanged implementation and governed inputs must be
verified before reassessment.
