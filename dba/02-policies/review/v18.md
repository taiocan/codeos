# Review Policy — v18

v18 incorporates v17 (VALIDITY WO-02, rule R5). A Stage 8 candidate assessment depends on its
Candidate Bundle's content identity (Feature Workflow v14) and on the selected reviewer tool
contract and review policy. It does not depend on the bytes of `codeos.yaml` or on another
checkpoint becoming stale.

Release-level assurance against latent defects is a separate decision and never affects feature
validity. Its scope and cadence are not defined in v18.
