# Review Policy — v14

v14 incorporates v13 in full and adds Bounded Replacement-Assessment Input Correction.

`ASSESSMENT_INPUT_CORRECTION` is available only when deterministic inspection of one consumed
`LINEAGE-REPLACEMENT-1` proves that every blocking finding arises exclusively from an objectively
incorrect identity or omitted inspectable evidence that already existed and was valid before the
assessment began. A substantive product, implementation, Specification Package, architecture,
safety, or integrity finding makes the operation unavailable.

The failed assessment, packet, `DO NOT ADVANCE` result, and consumed replacement remain immutable.
The correction is a separate append-only record bound to their identities, the exact corrected
inputs, proof of their pre-assessment existence, and one explicit human authorization.

After authorization, one deterministic preflight may permit exactly one
`LINEAGE-REPLACEMENT-CORRECTION-1`. It reassesses the same replacement question using only the
corrected inputs. Implementation, governed meaning, architecture, semantic revision, F6 selection,
and review scope MUST exactly match the failed replacement. New verification or evidence created
after the failed assessment is prohibited. Any mismatch refuses execution before capacity is
consumed.

This operation is not R4, REENTRY-2, another lineage replacement, Terminal Review Closure, or a
semantic revision. One authorization permits one execution, regardless of outcome. Reviewer
disagreement, exhausted capacity, genuinely insufficient evidence at review time, and post-review
repairs never establish eligibility.
