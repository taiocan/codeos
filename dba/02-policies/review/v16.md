# Review Policy — v16

v16 incorporates v15's readiness, machine-owned identity, scope-aware coverage, and execution
validity rules. It replaces semantic review capacity and recovery variants with one assessment per
immutable candidate.

The only outcomes are:

- `NOT_READY`: deterministic readiness failed; the reviewer is not invoked and no assessment exists.
- `EXECUTION_INVALID`: reviewer output is structurally invalid; raw output is preserved and the
  identical candidate may be re-executed.
- `BLOCKED`: one valid assessment found a substantive or evidence blocker; the same candidate cannot
  be assessed again.
- `PASSED`: one valid assessment found no progression blocker.

Further assessment after `BLOCKED` requires a different Candidate Bundle. There is no R1–R3 budget,
re-entry, closure, replacement, or correction operation. One valid semantic assessment per candidate
is the anti-bypass boundary. External assessments remain advisory.
