description: Reinforces the Reader Output policy selected by the project's DBA configuration.

State the core result first. Include only decision-relevant reasons and strongest direct evidence,
normally no more than three of each. Omit routine narration, unchanged details, and routine PASS
results. Define a semantics-critical specialized term on first use. Expand only when requested or
when omission could materially mislead the decision. The selected Reader Output policy remains
authoritative if this adapter differs from it.
