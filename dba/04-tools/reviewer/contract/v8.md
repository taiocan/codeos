# Reviewer Tool Contract — v8

Reviewer Tool Contract v8 incorporates Reviewer Tool Contract v7 in full and adds the terminal
Infrastructure Repair Revalidation defined by Review Policy v6.

`decision ... AUTHORIZE_INFRASTRUCTURE_REVALIDATION <reason> --after-review
<EVIDENCE-CLOSURE-1-id> --findings <finding-id>... --event-evidence-sha <sha256>` records the human
authorization. `review --infrastructure-revalidation-after <EVIDENCE-CLOSURE-1-id> --findings
<same-finding-id>... --event-evidence-sha <same-sha256>` consumes it and records exactly one
`REV__<feature>__<stage>__INFRA-REVALIDATION-1`.

Before provider invocation or durable writes, the tool validates the complete exhausted review
chain, negative closure result, exact authorization and finding set, absence of a later `STOP`,
single-use boundary, canonical runtime-event verification record, exact event-evidence artifact
hash, Event Schema conformance result, and equality of its implementation and governed-input
bindings with the reviewed verification history.

The packet is restricted to the named findings and infrastructure-repair evidence. An incomplete
assessment, scope or binding mismatch, unclosed finding, contradiction, or newly revealed blocker
records `DO NOT ADVANCE`. Only a complete assessment closing every named finding without another
blocker records `INFRASTRUCTURE EVIDENCE REVALIDATED — PROGRESSION PERMITTED`.

The record cites its closure boundary, human authorization, findings, and evidence hash. It does not
rewrite or reinterpret history, create a retry, approve a stage, accept a feature, or alter
checkpoint state by itself.
