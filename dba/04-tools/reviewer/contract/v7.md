# Reviewer Tool Contract — v7

Reviewer Tool Contract v7 incorporates Reviewer Tool Contract v6 in full and adds the Evidence
Closure Review defined by the selected Review Policy.

`decision ... AUTHORIZE_EVIDENCE_CLOSURE <reason> --after-review <REENTRY-1-id> --findings
<finding-id>...` records a single bounded authorization. `review --evidence-closure-after
<REENTRY-1-id> --findings <same-finding-id>...` consumes it and records
`REV__<feature>__<stage>__EVIDENCE-CLOSURE-1`.

The tool validates the feature and stage, consumed re-entry identity and result, non-empty exact
finding set, authorization order, absence of a later `STOP`, absence of an earlier closure, current
review bindings, and exact equality between authorized and reviewed findings before provider
invocation or durable writes. An incomplete assessment, scope mismatch, contradiction, blocker,
or binding drift records `DO NOT ADVANCE`; only a complete assessment closing every authorized
finding records `EVIDENCE CLOSED — PROGRESSION PERMITTED`.

The closure record cites its re-entry and authorization. It never rewrites prior records, resets
the ordinary round sequence, enables another re-entry or closure, approves a stage, or accepts a
feature.
