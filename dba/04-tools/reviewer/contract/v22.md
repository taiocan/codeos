# Reviewer Tool Contract — v22

v22 incorporates v21. CodeOS may project the coordinates of a GeoJSON LineString only at the
explicitly supported production-evidence field and schema versions. The projection is identified by
both its governed location and its declared semantic type. Every unsupported or ambiguous value is
shown whole, and the complete evidence stays bound by its SHA-256 and mechanically checked.

The only projection is `route-geometry/v1`. It applies to the input the Review Input Manifest
included as production evidence, with `schema: 1` or `2`, at
`observations[].visible_result.route.geometry`. CodeOS does not define `visible_result`; the
payload declares the semantics. The object must declare exactly `type: "LineString"`, with no
duplicate key on the path. Its `coordinates` must hold more than 16 positions, each two or three
numbers. Stage 8 then shows the coordinates as their count, first and last positions, bounding box,
16 evenly spaced samples including both ends, and the SHA-256 of the array's exact bytes. Every
other byte of the file, and every position shown, is the file's exact text.

The projected file keeps its content-addressed SHA-256 in the packet manifest, with mode
`projected`, its file and shown bytes, and the projection name. A projected required input is
inspectable. A feature that needs human review of route geometry introduces its own reviewable
artifact.
