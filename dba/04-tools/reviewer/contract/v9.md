# Reviewer Tool Contract — v9

Reviewer Tool Contract v9 retains Reviewer Tool Contract v6 and adds the Evidence Repair Loop from
Review Policy v7. Contracts v7 and v8 retain their historical operations; v9 refuses creation of
new Evidence Closure and Infrastructure Repair Revalidation records.

At F7, each `IN-SCOPE BLOCKER` must declare `Blocker kind: SUBSTANTIVE` or `Blocker kind:
EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`. An evidence-insufficient finding also declares `Evidence required:`. Missing
or malformed classification makes the assessment incomplete and cannot open the loop.

`review --evidence-reassessment-after <review-id> --findings <finding-id>... --event-evidence-sha
<sha256>` records `REV__<feature>__<stage>__EVIDENCE-REASSESSMENT-<N>`. It requires a valid eligible
predecessor, exact open finding scope, a current canonical runtime-event verification bound to the
same implementation and governed inputs, and evidence whose semantic identity has not already been
assessed for those findings. These checks occur before provider invocation or durable writes.

The packet limits the reviewer to whether the supplied evidence materially addresses and closes
the named findings. A complete `NO OBJECTION` closes the targeted findings. A complete negative
assessment keeps them open only when it reports materially relevant new evidence and the remaining
proof gap. A substantive finding closes loop eligibility. An incomplete assessment closes nothing
and does not consume the evidence identity.

The record cites its predecessor, findings, exact and semantic evidence identities, and verified
bindings. Reassessment is not an ordinary review round, a human decision, stage approval, product
acceptance, or authority to reinterpret history.
