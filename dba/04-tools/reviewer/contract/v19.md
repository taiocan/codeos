# Reviewer Tool Contract — v19

v19 incorporates v18. The same readiness, packet isolation, immutable identity, structural
validation, and four outcomes apply to `ordinary` and `post_acceptance_revalidation` Candidate
Bundles.

A revalidation candidate packet includes its immutable accepted baseline, trigger, affected
surface, unchanged governed meaning and architecture identity, dependency-scoped current inputs,
and required fresh evidence. The reviewer answers only whether that candidate remains conformant;
it cannot approve architecture, change meaning, create F9, or reopen historical findings.

The reviewer refuses legacy recovery options and any candidate whose purpose, baseline, trigger,
or current bindings are missing or inconsistent.
