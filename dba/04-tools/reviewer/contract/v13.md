# Reviewer Tool Contract — v13

Reviewer Tool Contract v13 retains v12 and implements Review Policy v11.

For a v13 closure execution, the tool parses the response before assigning its durable identity.
A malformed or incomplete first response records
`REV__<feature>__8__SR<revision>__TERMINAL-CLOSURE-FAILURE-1`; a malformed retry records the
corresponding `FAILURE-2`. Failure records contain only execution provenance, structural-failure
facts, immutable input bindings, and retry status. They have no semantic closure disposition and
cannot contribute substantive meaning to F7.

A structurally complete assessment records the existing deterministic
`REV__<feature>__8__SR<revision>__TERMINAL-CLOSURE-1`. Whether `OPEN` or `CLOSED`, it is final and
consumes the semantic closure. Legacy revision 0 omits the `SR` component as in v12.

`review --retry-terminal-review-closure-after <failure-review-id>` permits one retry using the
original authorization and saved semantic reviewer payload. It accepts no replacement scope,
repair manifest, or general packet-building input. Without an assessability correction the payload
is byte-identical. With `--assessability-correction <json>`, the tool appends only corrected or
supplemental evidence to the immutable payload.

The correction classification must be exactly `ASSESSABILITY_CORRECTION`. Its manifest binds the
failed review and assessment, original implementation identity, closure-scope identity, an exact
deficiency excerpt and SHA-256 from the failed assessment, and every corrected artifact to both its
superseded evidence identity and current SHA-256. Corrected artifacts must be evidence paths; they
cannot be governed artifacts, implementation, architecture, configuration, or unrelated evidence.
All unchanged artifact bindings are reverified. The packet identifies the immutable original packet
and includes the correction manifest plus sanitized inspectable evidence. The reviewer assesses
whether the correction only makes the original condition assessable; ambiguity fails closed.

A preserved malformed or incomplete v12 `TERMINAL-CLOSURE-1` may be the retry predecessor only
when the exact Review Policy v11 legacy conditions hold. Its one structurally complete retry uses
`REV__<feature>__8__SR<revision>__TERMINAL-CLOSURE-RETRY-1`; this identity is never used by a normal
v13 closure. A malformed legacy retry records `FAILURE-2` and exhausts retry capacity.

The tool refuses a retry after any complete assessment, changed binding, prior retry, second
failure, expanded scope, or later `STOP`. It never creates another review round, re-entry, semantic
revision, closure authorization, approval, or workflow progression.
