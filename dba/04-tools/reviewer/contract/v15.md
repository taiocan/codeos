# Reviewer Tool Contract — v15

v15 incorporates v14 in full.

Post-acceptance dependency revalidation uses the workflow tool's frozen revalidation packet. The
independent reviewer answers only `REVALIDATED` or `REVALIDATION FAILED` with a non-empty summary.
The workflow tool, not the reviewer, verifies F9 history, unchanged governed and architecture
meaning, trigger intersection, current implementation, and fresh evidence before packet creation
and again before recording. This assessment consumes no ordinary Stage-8 capacity and grants no
new acceptance authority.
