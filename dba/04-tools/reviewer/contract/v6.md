# Reviewer Tool Contract — v6

Reviewer Tool Contract v6 incorporates Reviewer Tool Contract v5 in full and adds the following
supported review operation and integrity guarantees.

## Bounded Review Re-entry Authorization

Ordinary reviews remain limited to `R1` through `R3`. After R3,
`review --reentry-after <R3-review-id>` permits one `REENTRY-1` review only when the selected Review
Policy permits re-entry and the append-only review log contains an `AUTHORIZE_REENTRY` decision
explicitly bound to that R3.

The referenced record must be the same feature and stage's R3, and no re-entry may already exist for
that feature and stage. `decision ... AUTHORIZE_REENTRY <reason> --after-review <R3-review-id>`
records the authorization and its non-empty reason/scope. This decision is review control only: it
does not approve a stage, advance a workflow, accept a product, or create a workflow receipt.

Records are evaluated in physical append order; timestamps never determine order. A later `STOP`
for the same feature and stage, an absent or mismatched authorization, and an already-consumed
authorization fail closed before provider invocation or durable writes. Other stage dispositions,
other features, other stages, and external assessments do not create or consume authorization.

The `REENTRY-1` record cites R3 and the authorization record's timestamp and bounded reason. Existing
records remain byte-for-byte unchanged. A consumed authorization permits exactly one additional
reviewer execution against its identified prior boundary and never changes stage state by itself.
Re-entry neither resets the ordinary round history nor permits another re-entry.
