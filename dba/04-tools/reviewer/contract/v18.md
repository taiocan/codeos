# Reviewer Tool Contract — v18

v18 incorporates v17's readiness and execution-validity rules. For Stage 8 it consumes the unique
current Candidate Bundle produced by `codeos-workflow prepare-candidate`; its execution manifest
references that bundle and may not re-derive conflicting identities.

The assessment identity is `ASSESS__<feature>__<stage>__CANDIDATE-<bundle-prefix>`. A candidate with a
valid `BLOCKED` or `PASSED` assessment cannot be invoked again. `EXECUTION_INVALID` may be retried
against identical bytes. Reviewer `NO OBJECTION`/`ADVANCE` maps to `PASSED`; any structurally valid
semantic blocker maps to `BLOCKED`.

DBA-23 rejects legacy recovery flags and authorization decisions before reviewer invocation. The
same binary retains their historical behavior only for projects selecting earlier contracts.
