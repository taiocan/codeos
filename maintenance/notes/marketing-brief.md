## CodeOS + DBA — Marketing Brief

**CodeOS** is a deterministic software-development framework for AI-assisted engineering.  
**DBA** is its governed delivery method: intent → contract → implementation → evidence → review → acceptance.

- **Human-gated AI development** — AI can implement and review, but human authority remains explicit.
- **Specification-first delivery** — features start from Intent, Contract, and Event Schema before implementation.
- **Evidence-based acceptance** — acceptance depends on verifiable runtime, test, browser, and review evidence.
- **Fail-closed governance** — missing, stale, contradictory, or incomplete evidence blocks progression.
- **Immutable workflow versions** — issued DBA configurations do not silently change meaning.
- **Replayable releases** — candidate workflow changes are qualified against compatibility and historical replay cases before issuance.
- **Feature-atomic control** — every feature has its own governed lifecycle, evidence, review, and acceptance state.
- **Shared validation** — one test, browser journey, production run, or human observation can prove multiple feature claims without merging feature ownership.
- **Claim-level traceability** — evidence is linked to explicit validation claims, not just broad test runs.
- **Stable system topology** — accepted product journeys remain structurally stable while evidence validity changes independently.
- **Production-safe verification** — external provider calls are accounted for, reconciled, and isolated from accidental execution.
- **Independent AI review** — reviewer judgments are advisory but formally bound to the exact candidate and evidence reviewed.
- **Historical integrity** — old evidence and approvals keep the semantics under which they were created.
- **Controlled revalidation** — unchanged evidence is reused; only genuinely affected claims or surfaces are rechecked.
- **Architecture and dependency awareness** — workflow validity includes configuration, implementation, verifier, evidence, and governed dependencies.
- **Deterministic status** — current validity, stale reasons, affected surfaces, and next actions are derived mechanically.
- **Audit-ready development** — decisions, evidence, reviews, configuration versions, and acceptance remain reconstructable.
- **Designed for AI-assisted teams** — reduces the risk of agents silently changing scope, requirements, or acceptance logic.
- **Optimized for continuous delivery** — ordinary features should move from specification to acceptance without changing the framework itself.

**Core proposition:**  
> CodeOS turns AI-assisted software development from an informal coding workflow into a governed, evidence-backed engineering process.

**DBA proposition:**  
> DBA makes every feature traceable from intended behavior to implementation, observed evidence, independent review, and human acceptance.
